Site-to-Site (S2S) VPN connections are a critical component of hybrid connectivity between Azure and on-premises environments. A tunnel outage can impact applications, user access, and business operations. Although Azure VPN Gateway automatically attempts to re-establish a tunnel when connectivity is restored, administrators should be notified immediately when a tunnel disconnects so they can quickly investigate and mitigate the issue.
